No 3Di substitution matrix is bundled. Load a published one (e.g. Q.3Di.AF, Puente-Lelievre et al.) with
strucevo.distances.load_paml(path, alphabet=THREEDI_ORDER) -- check the alphabet order of the source file! --
or estimate one from close pairs with strucevo.distances.estimate_gtr().
